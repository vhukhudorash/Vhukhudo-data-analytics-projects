# Power BI Projects

All five Power BI reports are kept directly in this folder so they are easy to find and download. The files are numbered to preserve the portfolio order.

## Projects

### 01 — Sales Dashboard (`01_Sales_Dashboard.pbix`)
A sales-performance dashboard with a separate insights and recommendations page. The report's insights page states total revenue of approximately R16.18 million, total profit of R3.48 million and total costs of R12.69 million. It identifies Pretoria as the highest-revenue city and highlights differences across product categories and stores. Recommendations include investigating lower sales in Soweto, maintaining stock for strong categories and reviewing weaker Home product performance. These figures are transcribed from the report's own insights page and have not been independently recalculated.

### 02 — Sales Report (`02_Sales_Report.pbix`)
A two-page sales report containing 19 visuals, including KPI cards, bar and line charts, a combo chart, scatter chart, ribbon chart, pie/donut charts, slicers and an action button. Open the PBIX in Power BI Desktop to explore the pages, filters and interactions.

### 03 — HR Dashboard (`03_HR_Dashboard.pbix`)
A workforce analytics dashboard with a separate insights and recommendations page. The report states 269 employees, 51 terminations and an attrition rate of approximately 16%. It highlights higher attrition in Marketing and Finance and recommends investigating turnover, strengthening retention measures and reviewing recruitment processes. Figures are transcribed from the report's own insights page and have not been independently recalculated.

### 04 — Financial Dashboard (`04_Financial_Dashboard.pbix`)
A financial performance dashboard with a separate insights and recommendations page. The report states revenue of approximately 7.42 million, net profit of 3.98 million, COGS of 1.80 million and operating expenses of 3.43 million. It identifies Quarter 2 as the strongest quarter and Quarter 4 as the weakest, recommending a review of the decline after Quarter 2 and ongoing monitoring of costs. Figures are transcribed from the report's own insights page and have not been independently recalculated.

### 05 — Marketing Dashboard (`05_Marketing_Dashboard.pbix`)
A marketing performance dashboard with a separate insights and recommendations page. The report states marketing spend of approximately 3.17 million, attributed revenue of 21.03 million, around 2 million engagements, 25 million impressions and 1.2 million clicks. It highlights channel and campaign performance and recommends reviewing channel budgets, studying Search Ads performance and improving Website marketing. Attributed revenue does not by itself establish that marketing activity caused the revenue. Figures are transcribed from the report's own insights page and have not been independently recalculated.

## How to view

Download any `.pbix` file and open it using Microsoft Power BI Desktop. GitHub does not render PBIX files as interactive dashboards in the browser.
